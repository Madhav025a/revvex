Copywriting Guidelines

The copy should be:

 Confident but never arrogant

 Benefit-focused rather than feature-focused

 Conversational and easy to read

 Free of marketing buzzwords and clichés

 Focused on outcomes: more consultations, more qualified leads, better online reputation, and higher conversion rates

Use claims such as guaranteed rankings or fixed numbers of appointments. like"we help clients get 7–8 appointments without spending any money on marketing,"
